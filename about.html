‘Why Do We Choose
To Go To The Moon?’

Psilos was founded by explorers.

We believe exploration and progress are inherently linked.

We support plans to explore the deepest depths of the ocean and the furthest solar systems in space.

Doing so requires intelligence. We aim to provide that for those at the frontier.

Mission Statement

At Psilos we believe that peace and progress are downstream of prosperity.

We believe that the number one constraint for economic growth in the current technological era is access to physical resources.

We aim to attack that constraint with ferocity and help unleash the next industrial revolution.

We believe that Earth is not an economic vessel to sell off to the highest bidder, but a sanctuary for humanity and all life that we must protect.

Capitalism currently separates from the preservation of natural resources because we have artificially incentivized the short-term over the long-term. Since this incentive structure has embedded itself into our capital markets, we aim to protect our cap table with only those who share our long-term vision for Earth & humanity.

We believe that human capital like natural capital has sanctity and must be treated with dignity and respect.

We always aim to maximize our potential — as individuals and an organization.

We will never give up the work of making the world a more prosperous place for all people.
